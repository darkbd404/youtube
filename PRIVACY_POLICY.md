# AS MASTER Ad Blocker - Privacy Policy
- No data is collected, uploaded or shared. The app declares no INTERNET permission.
- The Accessibility Service is limited to the official YouTube app (com.google.android.youtube) and only looks for a visible Skip Ads button.
- Statistics (daily counts of real successful clicks) are stored only on your phone and can be reset anytime.
- The app never changes your system DNS by itself.
